# Boulingua

Open language teaching and reading practice, created by **S. Le Boulanger**.
The suite serves teachers and learners, particularly in German schools, and
international learners of German.

[Explore the website](https://boulingua.github.io/website/) ·
[Suite architecture](../docs/suite-architecture.md) ·
[Current refinement and release limits](../docs/refinement-2026-10-30.md)

| Part of the suite | What it provides |
|---|---|
| [EFL](https://github.com/boulingua/efl) | 180 English units across school tracks and years |
| [FLE](https://github.com/boulingua/fle) | 156 French units across school tracks and years |
| [DaF](https://github.com/boulingua/daf) | 60 German units organised by CEFR level A1–C1 |
| [Ressources](https://github.com/boulingua/ressources) | A curated catalogue with German, English and French discovery views |
| [Scriptbridge](https://github.com/boulingua/scriptbridge) | Optional, reversible alphabet practice in a browser extension |
| [Scriptlibrary](https://github.com/boulingua/scriptlibrary) | 18 original-language books, browser reading and 288 PDF variants |
| [Kit](https://github.com/boulingua/kit) | Shared Hugo components, design tokens, fonts, printable materials, audio tooling and checks |
| [Curriculum](https://github.com/boulingua/curriculum) | Descriptor IDs, source records and evidence-based course mapping |
| [Organisation](https://github.com/boulingua/.github) | Architecture decisions, repository roles and coordinated workflows |

Fifteen further courses are in development: `afl`, `cfl`, `ele`, `gfl`, `ils`,
`jfl`, `lle`, `nsf`, `nvt`, `pfa`, `pfl`, `ple`, `rki`, `tfl` and `ufl`.
Their buildable foundations are not finished teaching curricula. Script support,
voice review and teaching scope follow the accepted architecture decisions.
The earlier `pagegen`, `slidegen`, `sheetgen` and `audiogen` repositories are
archived; their maintained successors are in Kit.

Existing courses have browser lessons and committed printable materials.
Recordings and curriculum assertions have separate provenance and review
requirements. Neither a file's presence nor a passing website build proves
educational completeness. Current audio and framework findings are recorded in
the refinement report, together with what has actually been tested.
