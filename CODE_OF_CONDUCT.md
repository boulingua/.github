# Code of conduct

This is a single-author open-educational-resources project. Most interaction
here is somebody reporting a mistake in teaching material, which is a
contribution and is treated as one.

**Be accurate and be kind, in that order of difficulty.** A report that a unit
teaches something wrong is welcome however bluntly it is put. Contempt for the
people using or making the material is not, however politely it is put.

The material is for language learners in German schools, many of them children,
and several courses are aimed at newcomers and heritage speakers. Content or
conduct that would make any of those learners less welcome here is out of
scope for this project, and that includes the recurring suggestion that a
particular language does not belong in a German school.

Enforcement is the maintainer's: comments may be edited or removed and accounts
blocked from the org. Report anything that needs attention to the contact
address on <https://boulingua.github.io/website/impressum/>.
